## Minimal implementation

Add a side-effect-free parser/validator module with one exported function per validation ID and a
small direct-execution CLI. It reports stable ID-prefixed diagnostics and rejects duplicate or
missing records. It checks exact inventory counts and unique IDs; D-001 through D-016 detail and
status completeness; unresolved markers, undefined references, placeholders, and invalid phase
references; and equality/completeness across all 43 finding register, detail, and traceability rows.
