## Recovery-proof plan amendment candidate

`P17-RV-G4A04-IMPL-F04` proves that the accepted declared-only unique-hash reconstruction is not
safe as written: the stale lock can reproduce the old manifest hash while still lying about its
full `resolvedSha` or `contentHash`. A store path carries only a 12-hex SHA prefix, and current
manifest/live/ledger absence supplies no independent full-revision authority. Repairing from that
state would let the stale artifact authorize deletion of its own unrelated drift.

The candidate amendment preserves exact lock-only recovery only when one fresh independent source
proves the selected entry's full SHA and content hash. Reduced recovery uses retained unselected
sibling pairs for the same declaration: exact manifest/source/scope agreement, lowercase full-40
`origin.refResolved === pinned.gitSha`, and pinned/live content equality are mandatory. Final
recovery proves every selected pair independently from its newest pair-affecting ledger history
entry by append position; that newest record itself must be one committed forward remove with
agreeing intent and actual present-before/absent-after images. Store bytes may corroborate content
but cannot prove the full SHA alone. Conflicting, superseded, rolled-back, ambiguous, nonterminal,
pruned, or missing authority refuses, and recovery never calls resolver, fetch, Git, or network
ports.

After that proof, the existing finite source/ref/tool/scope/placement/path reconstruction and exact
old manifest-hash match remain required. The material contract change is narrow but explicit:
history-free declared-only default-root handoff now refuses instead of repairing. The test matrix
replaces its prior declared-only-unique-hash acceptance with refusal and adds direct SHA/content
drift, retained-sibling reduced, per-selected-pair committed-history final, automatic reduced,
pending/nonterminal, rollback, later supersession, intent/actual disagreement, selected-pair
disagreement, ambiguous-owner, and zero-resolver/network rows. No mapping, dependency, ownership,
