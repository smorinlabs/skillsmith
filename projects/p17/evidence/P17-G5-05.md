## Accepted Ready amendment candidate

All five findings are accepted as blockers. The plan is amended without advancing lifecycle state:

- **F01:** each changing manifest or lock carrier declares a conditional retention resource,
  publishes its prepared journal under the existing locks, creates an owner-private durable backup
  envelope containing the exact regular-file bytes plus a canonical source-role/mode receipt,
  fsyncs it, records its complete digest in `actual.retained`, and only then invokes the existing
  artifact coordinator. The envelope digest authenticates the mode without adding a journal field.
  The pending logical journal remains the sole added recovery authority; the envelope is retained
  restoration material, not a second recovery format. Undo uses the validated retained bytes/mode
  rather than normalized reconstruction, and ordinary bounded-history cleanup owns eventual
  deletion.
- **F02:** inverse artifact operations remain group-level and pair-null. `group.operations`
  partitions into its artifact prefix plus the live-only pair vectors. Pair outcome stays pair-local;
  group outcome/failure reduces all group results. Strict negative codec coverage freezes omission,
  duplication, order, pair-binding, and artifact failure/cancellation truth without adding a public
  field or enum.
- **F03:** logical history gains an explicit schema-preserving artifact parent/child branch with no
  legacy shadow. Exact parent operation/transaction, path, images, retained backup, phase,
  command/workflow, and one-child rules are validated through prepared, resume, commit, and hostile
  cases.
- **F04:** the bounded selector treats the exact forward update occurrence—artifact carriers,
  correlated placement journals, rollback children, and retained resources—as one dependency
  unit. Protection expands effective capacity; exclusion and restartable cleanup remove the whole
  unit. More-than-256-entry, pending-child, two-tool, custom-pair, and cleanup-restart tests are
  mandatory.
- **F05:** update group identity adds a non-sensitive versioned digest of exact manifest/lock
  before/after identities. Historical correlation still starts from the selected placement source
  transaction and commit/image chain rather than assuming group IDs are globally unique. A->B->C->B,
  exact-repeat, different-trivia, intervening-history, two-tool, and custom-pair cases are mandatory.

The ownership manifest expands from 47 to 49 unique paths by adding the shared artifact execution
authority and its focused test. The existing planned `update/history.ts` and `undo/artifacts.ts`
boundaries own retention/correlation; the artifact coordinator remains the only portable-file
mutator. Public-process validation remains serialized. This amendment is a new Ready candidate and
requires structural validation, an immutable commit, and a fresh independent rerun before Ready
can pass.
